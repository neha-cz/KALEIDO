β-flatten

Loosely inspired by the Entropic Brain Hypothesis and REBUS, we lower the inverse temperature inside the attention mechanism, shallowing its Hopfield network energy landscape so the model drifts between patterns instead of committing to one.

Persona steer

Implementing Anthropic's method of persona vectors, we learn a direction in activation space that encodes ego dissolution and add it to the residual stream at every decode step, pulling the model's voice from inside, not from above.

Dream inject

Inspired by Google's DeepDream, we gradient-ascend the model's own visual feature geometry to generate surreal vectors, then inject them into the residual stream at every token, flooding language with a churning visual prior.
